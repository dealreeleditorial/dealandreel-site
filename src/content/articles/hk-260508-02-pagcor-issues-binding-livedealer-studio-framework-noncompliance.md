# PAGCOR Issues Binding Live-Dealer Studio Framework — Non-Compliance Means Market Exclusion

PAGCOR (Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation) has released a regulatory framework setting binding operational, technical, and compliance standards for live-dealer studios streaming to Philippine-licensed players, [according to CDC Gaming reporting from May 4, 2026](https://www.cdcgaming.com/). Studios that fail to meet the requirements face exclusion from the Philippine market.

The framework applies to any studio supplying live-dealer content to operators holding Philippine Internet Gaming Operator (IGO) licences — including third-party studios such as dedicated live-dealer providers and operator-owned facilities.

## What the Framework Requires

The specific operational, technical, and compliance requirements — including studio location rules, streaming quality standards, random number generator (RNG) certification obligations, and anti-money laundering (AML) controls — have not been independently confirmed against [PAGCOR's official regulatory circulars](https://www.pagcor.ph/) at the time of writing [verify against pagcor.ph memoranda section before publishing]. CDC Gaming's reporting indicates the framework covers both infrastructure and compliance controls, but the granular requirements should be treated as preliminary pending primary-document review.

The exact compliance deadline stated in the framework [verify] and whether existing licensed studios are grandfathered or must comply from the effective date [verify] are likewise unconfirmed from primary sources. PAGCOR has not publicly issued accompanying guidance or FAQs as of the reporting date.

## Who Is Affected

Downstream liability sits with IGO licensees as well as studios directly. Philippine-licensed online operators are responsible for ensuring their studio suppliers meet PAGCOR's standards — meaning operators cannot treat non-compliant studio relationships as a supplier problem alone. Technology vendors providing streaming infrastructure or studio software may also face pressure to update systems to meet any technical specifications in the framework.

Dedicated live-dealer providers with existing PAGCOR-market exposure — including large-scale studio operators active across Asian-facing platforms — will need to assess their compliance posture against the new standards once the primary document is available.

## What to Watch

PAGCOR has progressively tightened operational standards for its IGO licensee base over the past several years. This framework continues that pattern and signals that live-dealer supply chains — not just front-end operators — are now within the regulator's direct compliance perimeter. Operators should obtain the primary framework document from pagcor.ph and begin gap assessments immediately; waiting for further regulatory guidance before acting carries deadline risk.
