*Stockholm-listed live-casino supplier posts first double-digit-teens growth quarter since early 2024; shares gain about 5% after hours.*

Evolution AB (STO: EVO) reported Q1 2026 revenue up 18% year-on-year, according to the company's interim report released Thursday. The figure is a meaningful re-acceleration from the low-teens pace Evolution delivered through most of 2025 and came in above the range sell-side analysts had carried into the print. Shares rose roughly 5% in after-hours trade on Nasdaq Stockholm.

The Stockholm-based company is the dominant pure-play supplier of live dealer casino content to regulated online operators worldwide, with customers that include Flutter Entertainment, DraftKings, Entain, and Bet365. Its quarterly print is read as a proxy for the health of online live-casino demand, a segment that decelerated through 2024 and 2025 as European markets matured and regulatory friction rose.

## What drove the beat

Evolution did not disclose a full segment breakdown in the summary release reviewed here, and a detailed read-through awaits the earnings call transcript and the full interim report on the company's investor site. Analysts covering the stock will be watching three line items in particular: the growth rate of the Live Casino segment in isolation, the performance of the RNG segment that the company built through its acquisitions of NetEnt and Red Tiger, and the geographic split between North America, Europe, Asia, and Latin America.

The 18% figure is the first time since early 2024 that Evolution has posted a growth rate above the mid-teens, and it reverses a sequential deceleration that had become the central bear case on the stock. Management had guided to a growth rate in the low to mid-teens for the year.

## The overhangs that remain

A single quarter does not resolve the structural questions on Evolution. The company continues to face scrutiny over the share of its revenue sourced from markets without local licensing regimes — an issue raised repeatedly by Dutch and UK regulators through 2024 and 2025. Competitive pressure from Pragmatic Play Live, Playtech Live, and Stakelogic Live has intensified in Europe and Latin America, and Evolution's margin trajectory in 2026 will depend in part on how aggressively it responds on price and product.

Evolution did not update its full-year 2026 guidance alongside the Q1 release. Martin Carlesund, the company's chief executive, is scheduled to host the analyst call later Thursday.

## Takeaway

A single 18% print does not re-rate the stock by itself, but it forces a re-underwrite of the deceleration thesis that has weighed on Evolution since mid-2024. Watch three things on the call: the Live Casino organic rate ex-RNG, any disclosure on regulated-market revenue share, and whether management raises the full-year guide. The read-through for B2B-dependent operators — Flutter, DraftKings, Entain — is marginally positive on live-casino demand, but this is a supplier print, not an operator print, and direct extrapolation should be avoided.
