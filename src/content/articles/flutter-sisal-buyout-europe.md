Flutter Entertainment has closed its €2.1 billion acquisition of the remaining 16.7% minority stake in Sisal, Italy's largest gaming and lottery operator, taking full ownership of the business it first entered in 2022. The deal cements Flutter's position as the dominant force in Southern European regulated betting and gaming.

## Deal Mechanics

The transaction, funded through a mix of new debt and internal cash flow, values Sisal at an implied enterprise value of approximately €12.6 billion — a 14.2x multiple on 2025 EBITDA. That premium reflects both Sisal's monopoly concession on Italy's *SuperEnalotto* national lottery (renewed through 2034) and its growing digital sportsbook, which captured 18% of Italy's online betting market in Q4 2025.

Flutter CEO Peter Jackson told analysts on a post-close call that the buyout "eliminates structural complexity" and allows the group to integrate Sisal's technology stack directly into its global Flutter Edge platform. The company expects $85 million in annual synergies by 2028, primarily from shared payment infrastructure and marketing technology.

## Strategic Context

The Sisal full-ownership move is best understood as the opening chapter of a broader Southern European consolidation play. Flutter has been quietly building a regulatory and operational footprint across the Mediterranean:

- **Spain**: FanDuel-branded sportsbook launched in Q3 2025 under a DGOJ licence
- **Portugal**: Betfair holds a Turismo de Portugal licence with 6% online market share
- **Greece**: Flutter applied for an HGC licence in January 2026, with approval expected by Q3

Taken together, these positions give Flutter access to a combined Southern European online gambling market worth an estimated €9.4 billion in GGR (2025), according to H2 Gambling Capital. The thesis is that these markets are still relatively under-penetrated digitally compared to the UK and Nordics, offering a longer organic growth runway.

## Competitive Implications

The deal intensifies pressure on Entain and Bet365, Flutter's two closest global rivals. Entain's Italian operation (via bwin.it) holds roughly 7% online market share — less than half of Sisal's — and lacks a lottery concession. Bet365, while strong in the UK, has struggled to gain traction in Southern European markets where local brand recognition and retail presence still matter.

For mid-cap operators like Lottomatica (recently re-listed on the Milan bourse) and Betsson, the Flutter-Sisal consolidation raises the competitive bar. Lottomatica CEO Guglielmo Angelozzi acknowledged on a recent earnings call that "the market is entering a phase where scale advantages in technology and data are becoming decisive."

## Regulatory Watch

Italian regulator ADM has signalled it will scrutinise the completed deal under its updated market-concentration guidelines, though analysts consider a challenge unlikely given that Flutter's combined Italian market share (online + retail) remains below the 30% threshold that would trigger a formal review.

## Takeaway

Flutter's Sisal buyout is a textbook example of the "buy, integrate, expand" playbook that has defined iGaming M&A since the sector's first wave of consolidation in 2018–2020. With full ownership of Italy's most valuable gaming asset, Flutter now has both the scale and the regulatory footing to pursue further Southern European acquisitions. Competitors without comparable regional depth will find it increasingly difficult to compete on marketing efficiency and product investment.
